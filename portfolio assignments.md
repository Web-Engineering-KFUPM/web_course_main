# SWE 363 - 261 AI-assisted Protfolio Assignments

The purpose of these course assignments is to provide you aspiring developers with practical experience in web development through hands-on projects that incorporate AI-assisted development techniques. Each assignment builds upon the previous one, creating a comprehensive learning experience that prepares you for real-world web development challenges.

Step by step, you will transform a blank page into a professional online presence. Along the way, you will practice the same skills web developers use daily:

- Writing clean and responsive code.
- Adding interactive features that bring pages to life.
- Connecting your site to real data and services.
- Documenting your work so others can follow and understand it.

Approach each assignment with creativity, curiosity, and a commitment to learning. Use AI tools as partners in your development journey, but always ensure you understand the code you're writing and the concepts you're implementing. The skills you develop through these assignments will serve as a strong foundation for your future web development career.

Remember, the goal is not just to complete the assignments, but to truly master the concepts and create something you're proud to showcase.

By the end of this series of assignments, you will have:

- A complete portfolio website to showcase to recruiters, supervisors, or collaborators.
- Practical experience in AI-assisted development used responsibly.
- The confidence that comes from building something yourself, with AI as your partner, not your replacement.
- A testament to your growth and capabilities as a web developer.

## AI Usage Declaration (Required for All Assignments)
- AI use is encouraged in these assignments, and every use must be declared.
- Each assignment's `docs/ai-usage-report.md` must include a **Usage Log** and a signed **Declaration**, and the full AI conversations and intermediate artifacts must be submitted on Blackboard as an **AI Evidence ZIP**.
- **A missing declaration or evidence results in a grade of 0** for the assignment.
- A false declaration or undeclared AI use will be treated as a **plagiarism case** according to KFUPM regulations.
